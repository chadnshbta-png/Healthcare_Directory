Official licensing data

      
Categories, specialties, licence types and facility links come straight from the public register — no scraped guesses layered on top.

    

    

      

        
      

      
Transparent about gaps

      
If the register does not publish a field, we leave it out rather than filling it in. A missing value here means missing at source.
